TREASURAR 1.0.2
========================================

INSTALL
1. Double-click TREASURAR-Setup.exe and choose Yes.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info -> Run anyway.
2. Installing over an earlier version keeps your data.

ACCOUNT TYPES
Regular - for everyone:
  - Up to 5 accounts
  - Live prices, budgets, invoices and receipts
  - Net-worth history up to 4 months

Premium - 4 USDC, one-time, from Chillproducts:
  - Up to 50 accounts
  - Full 1-year net-worth history
  - CSV exports of assets and reports
  - Institutional dashboard: TVL, AUM, client mandates
  - Locked/staked positions and concentration alerts
  - Premium settings: alert threshold, default chart range, dashboard summary

GETTING PREMIUM
- Premium is a one-time purchase from Chillproducts: 4 USDC, or the same value in SOL or ETH. Your product key works for good.
- On the first screen choose Premium and paste your product key.
- Already using Regular? Click the Regular badge at the top, paste the key, click Activate Premium.
- Premium settings: Settings -> Premium tab.
- Your key works only for this app. Keep it safe; you can use it again if you reinstall.

WORKS OFFLINE
- Everything runs on this computer and your data stays here. Only live crypto prices and currency rates need the internet.
- Use Export backup now and then.

AI agent (TREASURAI) is in beta and not included yet.
